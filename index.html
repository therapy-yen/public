Meet your therapist

Hi, I'm Yen.

I'm a Licensed Mental Health Counselor working with people who are ready to break old cycles. Many of my clients grew up holding roles they never chose: the responsible one, the peacekeeper, the one who made it. Those roles helped you survive. They may also be the reason you feel so tired.

In our work, we look for the root causes of your distress and the patterns that keep your system on high alert. We work on your defenses and survival strategies without asking you to leave behind your identity, culture, or heritage. Those stay part of the story.

I won't only ask how you feel. I'll show you what's happening in your brain and body, so change feels understandable and doable.

Areas of Focus

Where we can direct our work

Individual telehealth therapy for adults in New York and Vermont, with a focus on three connected areas.

  • Relational dynamics

    The roles you learned in your family often show up again in friendships, partnerships, and work. We map those patterns so you can respond on purpose instead of on autopilot.

  • Trauma

    Trauma is stored in the body as well as in memory. You'll learn how your nervous system learned to protect you, and how to update the strategies that no longer fit your life.

  • Anxiety

    When saying "no" feels dangerous, your alarm system is working overtime. We build practical skills to quiet it, so boundaries and conflict start to feel safe.

My approach

Neuro-informed and structural

Understanding why you react is the first step toward reacting differently. Each session combines insight with skills you can actually use.

  1. 01

    Understand the why

    Clear explanations of how stress and trauma shape the brain and body, so your reactions finally make sense.

  2. 02

    Name the pattern

    Quick pattern recognition to name the roles, structures, and survival strategies that keep you on high alert.

  3. 03

    Practice the change

    Concrete skill-building that retrains your nervous system to hold boundaries and move through conflict safely.
